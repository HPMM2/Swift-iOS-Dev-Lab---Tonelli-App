## 💡 The Problem

Data centers consume enormous amounts of energy, and they grow fast — a single server can scale from 1TB to 10TB in just 6 months. The AI that's supposed to help us be more sustainable... is itself part of the problem.

**Tonalli was born from one question: what if the AI helping you save energy didn't consume it all in the process?**

---

## 📱 What is Tonalli?

Tonalli is an iOS app with two core features:

### 🏠 Smart Home Control Center
Control all your smart devices from a single app. Forgot to turn off the AC before leaving? One tap and it's done — from anywhere. Compatible with **HomeKit** and other smart devices regardless of brand. Includes dark mode support.

### 🌱 EcoLearn — Distributed AI Training
Instead of relying on massive energy-hungry servers, EcoLearn uses the processing power of users' own devices to train AI models — a distributed system that reduces data center load and prevents exponential infrastructure growth.

- ✅ Runs only in the background while your device is **charging**
- ✅ Requires **explicit user authorization** to activate
- ✅ **No private data** is ever collected or shared
- ✅ Fully **open-source**
- ✅ Third parties can contract the service to train their own AI models
